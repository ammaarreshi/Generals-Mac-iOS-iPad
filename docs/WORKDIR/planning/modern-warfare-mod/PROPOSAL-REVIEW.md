# Review of PROPOSAL.md ("Zero Hour: Kill Chain")

Reviewer: independent pass, 2026-10-22. Method: read the proposal and the fact-check, then checked each cited engine mechanism in `GeneralsMD/Code/GameEngine/Source` and each cited template against the extracted retail INIs (`.../scratchpad/ini/Data/INI/`). Nothing in the proposal, the game or any INI was modified.

**Verdict: Not ready to build as written. Fix 3 blockers and 8 majors first.** Most of the INI grounding and all command-set budgets are accurate, and the engine facts about EMP, PDL, MissileAI and ArmorUpgrade are right. The weak points are (1) the subdual "selective soft-kill" idea, which does not work because of how healing is scheduled, (2) the `AURORA_BOMB` repurposing, whose "armors default to 100 %" safety claim is false in the retail data, and (3) three carrier/spawner features that have no retail precedent and miss a hard engine requirement.

Paths: `E:` = `GeneralsMD/Code/GameEngine/Source/GameLogic/Object`; `I:` = retail INI root (`Data/INI`).
