Compiled 2026-10-22 from public, open sources. Capability-level descriptions only. Items marked **unverified** could not be confirmed with a 2025-26 source during this pass. ## 1. Overview: US doctrine and character, 2025-26

The US military in 2025-26 is a force in deliberate, contested transition. Its identity is still long-range precision strike, stealth air power, space/ISR and a global logistics reach, but ground forces are being rebuilt around lessons from Ukraine and the Middle East: drones are ubiquitous, armor and bases are vulnerable from above, GPS and radios are jammed, and exquisite interceptors cannot be spent on cheap drones. The Army Transformation Initiative (ATI, Apr-May 2025) cancelled or capped legacy buys (Humvee, JLTV, AH-64D, Gray Eagle, M10 Booker) and prioritized long-range missiles, counter-drone defense, mass drones, AI-assisted command and a lighter "mobile" infantry model [S1][S2][S3]. The Pentagon's July 2025 "drone dominance" memo and the Army's goal of buying roughly one million drones in 2-3 years push mass-produced attritable systems [S15][S16]. Meanwhile, Operation Epic Fury (Iran, from 28 Feb 2026; ceasefire 7 Apr) showed both US strengths (deep strike, air power, first combat use of PrSM) and a hard limit: munitions and interceptor stockpiles drained faster than industry can replace them [S14]. ATI itself is now under review: Gen. George was replaced and Hegseth said it needs "another look" [S5]. The game-relevant character: **a high-tech, expensive, precision-fires-and-air-power faction that is transitioning toward cheap mass drones and layered counter-drone defense, with a glass jaw in stockpile depth, base vulnerability and EW dependence.**

## 3. Signature strengths and vulnerabilities

**Strengths (what should define US play)**
- **Deep precision strike**: PrSM (400-500 km, 2 per HIMARS pod), Dark Eagle (~2,775 km), Typhon (SM-6/Tomahawk), Tomahawk salvos. Hits infrastructure and command nodes from outside enemy reach. Combat-proven in Epic Fury [S12][S14].
- **Stealth and air dominance**: F-35, F-22, B-21, B-2 with GBU-57 bunker busters; the June 2025 Fordow strike showed penetrating power against hardened sites [S27].
- **Layered counter-drone/air defense**: Coyote + radar (LIDS), M-SHORAD, MADIS, microwave and laser prototypes, Patriot/THAAD above. Cheap interceptors are the point [S21].
- **Recon-strike networks**: squad drones (X10D), loitering munitions (Switchblade), SIGINT/EW detection, space ISR, AI-assisted command. Information advantage turns into fast fires.
- **Survivable next-gen armor**: M1E3 with active protection (Iron Fist-derived), lighter, 3 crew; Stryker 30 mm for mobile firepower [S7][S9].
- **Mass drones emerging**: LUCAS one-way attack drones, CCA wingmen, one-million-drone goal. The US is adopting cheap mass, though not yet at Ukrainian or Iranian scale [S16][S27].
- **Marine littoral kit**: NMESIS, MADIS, OPF-L give small, dispersed units outsized anti-ship and anti-air reach [S22].

**Vulnerabilities (what should limit it)**
- **Stockpile depth**: Epic Fury burned through Tomahawk, THAAD and Patriot stocks that will take years to rebuild; DoD acknowledged "strategic inventory shortfalls" [S14]. In-game: expensive weapons need limited charges, long cooldowns or resource costs.
- **Cost asymmetry**: a ~$4M PAC-3 against a ~$20-35K drone loses the exchange; only cheap interceptors fix it [S14][S21].
- **Fixed bases are exposed**: hundreds of structures and dozens of aircraft hit across Gulf bases; AN/TPY-2 radar sites damaged [S14].
- **GPS/EW dependence**: heavy reliance on satellite navigation, data links and radios; jamming degrades precision and drones (alt-PNT is still being fielded) [S30].
- **Thin light forces after cuts**: no new Humvee/JLTV or AH-64D, aviation personnel cut, Booker gone; light infantry rely on ISV and drones [S1][S28].
- **Exquisite platform scarcity and delays**: F-35 Block 4 delay, F-47 not flying until 2028, CCA still entering service, XM30 and M1E3 not yet in production [S19][S8].
- **Drone attrition at range**: MQ-9s are shot down by modest air defenses; legacy large UAS do not survive contested air [S26].
- **Policy and funding churn**: ATI under review, leadership change, reconciliation-dependent budgets [S5]. Fits a "tech tree that changes" feel.
