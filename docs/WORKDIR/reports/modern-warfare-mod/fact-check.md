# Fact-check of the three modern-warfare-mod research reports

Checked 2026-10-22 by web search and page fetch against research-usa.md, research-china.md and research-irregular-and-trends.md. The research files were not edited.

Method and limits: each claim was searched for corroboration in outlets such as Reuters-syndicated copy, AP, Defense News, Breaking Defense, The War Zone (TWZ), DefenseScoop, CSIS, Army, CENTCOM, CRS and the CTC Sentinel. Several pages were read in full (Breaking Defense IG story, GovExec, TWZ CSIS stockpile and M1E3 and Jiu Tian pieces, FAS, CRS JLTV, the CTC Sentinel Rough Rider PDF). Others were seen only as search summaries, and those rows say so. Many Epic Fury figures are think-tank estimates, because the Pentagon has not released inventory counts.

Verdict key: Confirmed, Partly confirmed, Unconfirmed (no corroboration found), Contradicted (sources say otherwise).
